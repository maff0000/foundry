## Definition states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Defined | Capability and value are sufficiently understood for further refinement; not automatically implementation-ready. |
| Provisional | Capability is understood, but important assumptions, dependencies or decisions remain unresolved. |
| Placeholder | A future capability is anticipated, but detailed definition is premature. Agents must not invent requirements from this entry. |

Use the state that reflects available understanding. Unknown priority or detail may remain explicitly unknown. Future capabilities do not require detailed specifications up front.
